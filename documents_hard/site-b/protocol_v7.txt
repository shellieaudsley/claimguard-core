OBSERVATION PHASE PROTOCOL — VERSION 7
Riverside Trust | Research Operations | RT-RO-OP-007
Status: in force | Supersedes: version 6

1. PURPOSE
Operational summary of version 7 as applied at this trust. Where this document
and the master protocol differ, the master protocol governs and the difference
is logged as a deviation.

2. GOVERNANCE
Adopted following review by the trust research committee. Adoption was
conditional on the monitoring arrangements in section 3 and on the medication
restrictions in section 4 being applied without local variation.

3. OBSERVATION SCHEDULE
Summary of v7 as it applies here. Bulleted because the source document is a table and I have flattened it.

- observation phase: reviewed on a fixed schedule
  - minimum monitoring interval: 6 hours
  - deviations: must be logged

Concurrent anticoagulant therapy is not permitted at any point in the observation phase. This holds irrespective of prior stabilisation, so it is not a judgement call.

Where a participant's condition changes unexpectedly, it is the duty registrar who assumes responsibility for the clinical decision.
5. DEVIATIONS
Every deviation is logged with a reason code and reviewed at the weekly
operations meeting. Reason codes are held in appendix B.

6. CONTACTS
Operational queries go to research operations. Clinical queries go to the site
investigator.
