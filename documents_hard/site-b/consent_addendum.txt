CONSENT PROCEDURE — ADDENDUM
Riverside Trust | Research Governance
Addendum to the master consent procedure. Read in conjunction with it.

1. TIMING
Participants get a minimum of 24 hours to consider the information sheet before consent is sought.

A witnessed signature is required where the participant cannot read the sheet unaided.
2. FILING
Completed consent forms are filed in the investigator site file and a copy is
given to the participant. A third copy is retained in the participant's
clinical record where local policy requires it.
