SPECIMEN HANDLING — RIVERSIDE TRUST
SOP-RT-009 | Laboratory Services | Revision 3

1. SCOPE
Receipt, storage and transfer of study specimens at this trust.

2. STORAGE AND TRANSFER
Plasma specimens are kept under refrigeration pending transfer; ambient storage is not acceptable for this analyte.

Transfer to the central laboratory runs twice daily, at 11:00 and 17:30.
3. QUERIES
Queries regarding specimen condition on receipt are directed to the duty
biomedical scientist, who records the query and the outcome.
