DUTY ROSTER — ESCALATION ARRANGEMENTS
Eastfield Unit | Published weekly

1. FIRST LINE
Unscheduled deterioration is escalated to the on-call consultant in the first instance.

The consultant may delegate to a senior registrar where the situation is judged stable — that is a delegation, not a transfer of authority.
2. DELEGATION
Delegation arrangements are recorded in the roster notes.

3. PUBLICATION
The roster is published weekly. Swaps are agreed between the individuals
concerned and notified to the unit sister.
