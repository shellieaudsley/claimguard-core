LABORATORY ANNEX — SPECIMEN STORAGE
Eastfield Unit | Annex to SOP-LAB-014
This annex records where local practice differs from the parent SOP, and why.

1. STORAGE CATEGORY
Owing to the distance from the central laboratory, the plasma specimen storage category at this unit is frozen.

Specimens are batched and dispatched three times weekly rather than daily.
2. MONITORING
Storage equipment is temperature-logged continuously; excursions trigger an
alert to the on-call biomedical scientist.

3. RATIONALE
The arrangement in section 1 reflects transport time to the central laboratory
and was agreed with the parent laboratory at adoption.
