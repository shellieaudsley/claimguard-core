OBSERVATION PHASE PROTOCOL — VERSION 3
Northgate General NHS Foundation Trust | Research Governance Office
Document ref: NGH-RGO-OP-003 | Supersedes: version 2 (withdrawn)
Distribution: site investigators, senior nursing staff, pharmacy, laboratory

1. PURPOSE AND STATUS
This document sets out observation-phase procedures in force at this site. It
is read alongside the master protocol and does not vary it except where this
document says so explicitly. Where the two conflict and this document is silent
on the conflict, the master protocol governs.

2. SCOPE
Applies to every participant enrolled after the effective date recorded in
section 4. Participants enrolled under version 2 continue on the version 2
schedule until their observation phase completes; they are not transitioned
mid-phase.

3. ROLES
The site investigator holds overall responsibility. Day-to-day conduct of the
observation phase is delegated to the senior nurse on duty. Pharmacy holds
responsibility for concurrent medication review and is consulted before any
change to a participant's regular therapy.

4. OBSERVATION SCHEDULE
Right, I've been through the v3 protocol end to end. Most of it is unchanged from v2, so I'll only flag what differs.

Observations during the observation phase are meant to run every four hours at minimum — that seems to be the floor, though staff can go tighter where they judge it necessary.

The version came into force on 15 January 2026 and supersedes every schedule circulated before that date.

On concurrent medication:
  - anticoagulant therapy is permitted, provided the participant was already stabilised on a maintenance dose
  - dose changes mid-study need sign-off
5. RECORD KEEPING
Entries are made in the participant record contemporaneously. Retrospective
entry is a recurring audit finding and is not acceptable. Deviations are logged
in the deviation register and reviewed monthly by the governance office.

6. TRAINING
Staff complete protocol-specific training before working unsupervised on the
observation ward. Training records are held by the research nurse.

7. REVIEW
Reviewed annually, or on amendment of the master protocol, whichever falls
sooner. Amendments are circulated to the distribution list above and the
superseded version withdrawn from the shared drive on the day the amendment
takes effect.
