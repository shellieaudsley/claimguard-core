MEMORANDUM
To: Observation ward nursing staff, night co-ordinators
From: Clinical lead, observation phase
Subject: Escalation of unscheduled deterioration
Classification: internal — not for circulation outside the trust

This memo restates existing practice following two incidents in which the
escalation route was unclear to staff covering at night. It introduces no new
pathway and does not amend the protocol.

BACKGROUND
Both incidents involved participants whose condition changed between scheduled
reviews. In neither case was there harm. In both, the delay arose from
uncertainty about who to contact rather than from clinical judgement.

ROUTE
Where a participant deteriorates outside a scheduled review, the matter goes to the on-call consultant without delay, and it is they who hold decision authority.

Nursing staff document the referral in the record at the time it is made. Retrospective entries are a recurring audit finding.
RECORD KEEPING
The referral is documented in the participant record at the time it is made.

QUERIES
Questions about this memo go to the clinical lead. Questions about a specific
participant go through the normal clinical route and not via this memo.
