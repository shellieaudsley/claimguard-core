STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE — SPECIMEN HANDLING
Northgate General Laboratory | SOP-LAB-014 | Revision 6
Approved by: Laboratory Director | Next review: annual

1. SCOPE
Covers receipt, labelling, storage and dispatch of study specimens. Analytical
method is held separately in SOP-LAB-021 and is out of scope here.

2. RECEIPT AND LABELLING
Specimens are checked against the request form on receipt. Discrepancies in
participant identifier, collection time or sample type are recorded and the
collecting ward contacted before the specimen is processed. Unlabelled
specimens are not processed under any circumstances.

3. STORAGE
The lab SOP is mostly logistics, but there is one substantive line in it.

Plasma is held chilled rather than frozen — the specimen storage category for plasma is refrigerated, and frozen is reserved for the long-term archive.

Courier collection is daily at 16:00, which is not something the protocol constrains.
4. DISPATCH
Specimens are logged out at the point of collection. The courier signs the
manifest and a copy is retained at the site for twelve months.

5. EQUIPMENT
Storage equipment is temperature-logged continuously. Excursions outside the
specified range trigger an alert to the on-call biomedical scientist, who
records the excursion and assesses whether affected specimens remain viable.

6. DEVIATIONS
Any deviation from this SOP is recorded on the laboratory deviation form and
reviewed at the monthly quality meeting.
