OBSERVATION WARD — STAFFING ESTABLISHMENT
Northgate General | Reviewed quarterly by the ward manager
Current review: Q1 | Next review: Q2

PURPOSE
Records the funded establishment for the observation ward. This is a planning
document. It does not authorise variation and is not a rota.

ESTABLISHMENT
Day shifts on the observation ward run at one nurse to six participants. Nights are covered differently and I have not looked at that in detail.
NIGHT COVER
Night shift arrangements are held separately in the rota and are not part of
the funded day establishment recorded here.

SHORTFALLS
Escalation of staffing shortfalls follows the trust-wide policy and is out of
scope for this document.
